# Don't Think About

Topics the owner has already decided and does not want raised again as doubts, risks or
suggestions during analyses. Agents and assistants must skip them.

## 1. Closed topics

| Topic | Decision |
|-------|----------|
| Crowdfunding rewards inside the game | Not planned; no backer tables or in-game rewards |
| Licensing of stories written by third parties | Known by the owner; no CLA analysis needed |
| Maintenance branches and hotfix process | Urgent fixes are `X.42.z`, sequential, never planned in a roadmap |
| Cost of keeping three backends (Java, Python, AWS) | Accepted; all three stay aligned until the owner decides otherwise |
| Recovery of a lost SSO account | The user's responsibility; no recovery flow |
| Timing of the owner's alpha story | Already in progress, ready for step 42 |
| Mobile browser support of react-game | Already working |
| Scope of the tutorial | Decided by the owner |
