3 · Production notes

Formats

  • Story: 1080×1920, keep text inside the middle 80% (stickers eat top/bottom)
  • Feed: 1080×1350 (4:5) — the tallest format the grid still crops well
  • Story frames export at 10 s each; total 60 s

Motion

  • Cards enter with the game's own hover move: translateY(-3px) scale(1.03)
  • Chosen card: gold glow ramp over 0.4 s, others fade to 35%
  • Effect pills (−2 ⚡, +1 Key) tick in one by one, 0.2 s apart

Stickers

  • Frame 5: poll — River or ruins?
  • Frame 6: link sticker, plus “Play free” text
  • No music with lyrics under the 30–45 s emotion beat

Assets & credits

  • All images and copy come from tutorial_story.json (English texts)
  • Photos: Unsplash — credit per card in the story's linkCopyright
  • Colours/fonts: react-game variables.css + main.css
